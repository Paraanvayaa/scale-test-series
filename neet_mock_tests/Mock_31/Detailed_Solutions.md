### Question 3
**Subject:** Physics | **Section:** Section A | **Chapter:** Motion in a Plane and Projectiles (Velocity Vector Perpendicular to Initial Velocity)
**Question Type:** Direct MCQ
**Correct Answer:** Option **(1)**

#### Step-by-Step Pedagogical Explanation:
1. Velocity vector at time $t$: $\vec{v}(t) = (u\cos\theta)\hat{i} + (u\sin\theta - gt)\hat{j}$.
2. Initial velocity: $\vec{u} = (u\cos\theta)\hat{i} + (u\sin\theta)\hat{j}$.
3. For $\vec{v} \perp \vec{u}$, the dot product vanishes: $\vec{v} \cdot \vec{u} = 0$.
   $$u^2\cos^2\theta + u\sin\theta(u\sin\theta - gt) = 0 \implies u^2(\cos^2\theta + \sin^2\theta) - ugt\sin\theta = 0$$.
   $$u^2 - ugt\sin\theta = 0 \implies t = \frac{u}{g\sin\theta}$$.
4. Substituting values: $t = \frac{20}{10 \times \sin 60^\circ} = \frac{2}{\sqrt{3}/2} = \frac{4}{\sqrt{3}}\text{ s} \approx 2.31\text{ s}$.

#### Distractor & Option Deconstruction:
Distractors use total time of flight $T = \frac{2u\sin\theta}{g} = 2\sqrt{3}\text{ s}$ or divide by $\cos\theta$.

**Key NCERT Principle / High-Yield Takeaway:** Velocity becomes perpendicular to initial projection velocity at $t = \frac{u}{g\sin\theta}$.
