**Q5.** One mole of a monatomic ideal gas is taken through a cycle $1 \rightarrow 2 \rightarrow 3 \rightarrow 1$ where $1 \rightarrow 2$ is isobaric expansion, $2 \rightarrow 3$ is isochoric cooling, and $3 \rightarrow 1$ is isothermal compression:
