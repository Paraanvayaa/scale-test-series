## PART I: PHYSICS

### Q1. Thin Circular Ring on Incline
- **Analysis**:
  - $I = M R^2$. Kinetic energy: $E_k = \frac{1}{2} M v_0^2 + \frac{1}{2} (M R^2) \frac{v_0^2}{R^2} = M v_0^2$. (A is True)
  - Linear acceleration rolling up: $a = \frac{g \sin\alpha}{1 + I/(MR^2)} = \frac{g \sin\alpha}{1 + 1} = \frac{1}{2} g \sin\alpha$. (B is True)
  - Maximum height by energy conservation: $M g h = M v_0^2 \implies h = \frac{v_0^2}{g}$. (C is True)
  - Static friction: $f = M g \sin\alpha - M a = \frac{1}{2} M g \sin\alpha \le \mu M g \cos\alpha \implies \mu \ge \frac{1}{2} \tan\alpha$. (D is True)
- **Correct Options**: **(A, B, C, D)**.

### Q2. Sphere on Accelerated Belt
- **Analysis**:
  - Belt accelerates forward $\implies$ friction on sphere is forward: $f = M a_{cm}$. (A is True)
  - Torque about contact point: $M a_0 R = (I_{cm} + M R^2) \alpha = \frac{7}{5} M R^2 \left(\frac{a_{\text{rel}}}{R}\right)$.
  - Acceleration of CM: $a_{cm} = \frac{2}{7} a_0$, friction $f = \frac{2}{7} M a_0$, relative acceleration $a_{cm} - a_0 = -\frac{5}{7} a_0$. (B, C, D are True)
- **Correct Options**: **(A, B, C, D)**.

### Q3. Falling Block on Vertical Spring
- **Analysis**:
  - Energy conservation from drop point to maximum compression: $m g (h + x_{\max}) = \frac{1}{2} k x_{\max}^2$. (A is True)
  - Maximum speed occurs when net vertical force is zero: $m g = k x \implies x = m g / k$. (B is True)
  - At maximum compression: $F_{\text{net}} = k x_{\max} - m g \implies a_{\max} = \frac{k x_{\max}}{m} - g$. (C is True)
  - If attached, executes SHM about equilibrium $x = m g / k$. (D is True)
- **Correct Options**: **(A, B, C, D)**.

### Q4. Capillary Cut Tube and Variations

  - In free fall ($g_{\text{eff}} = 0$), surface tension pulls liquid to the top. (D is True)
- **Correct Options**: **(A, B, C, D)**.

### Q8. Inelastic Impact Velocity of CM
- **Derivation**:

### Q9. Tension in Pendulum at Angle

### Q11. Escape Velocity Ratio
- **Derivation**:
  $$v_e = \sqrt{\frac{2 G M}{R}} \implies \frac{v'_e}{v_e} = \sqrt{\frac{M'/M}{R'/R}} = \sqrt{\frac{8}{2}} = \sqrt{4} = 2$$
  $$v'_e = 2 \times 11.2 = 22.4\text{ km/s}$$
- **Answer**: **22.4**.

### Q12. Frequency of Closed Organ Pipe

- **Answer**: **125**.

### Q25. Nernst Potential of Galvanic Cell
- **Derivation**:
  - Cell reaction: $\text{Mg} + 2\text{Ag}^+ \rightarrow \text{Mg}^{2+} + 2\text{Ag}$.
  - $E^\circ = 0.80 - (-2.37) = 3.17\text{ V}$.
  - $Q = [\text{Mg}^{2+}] / [\text{Ag}^+]^2 = 0.10 / (0.010)^2 = 0.10 / 10^{-4} = 1000 = 10^3$.
  - $E = 3.17 - \frac{0.0591}{2} \log_{10}(10^3) = 3.17 - \frac{0.0591}{2}(3) = 3.17 - 0.0886 \approx 3.08\text{ V}$.
- **Answer**: **3.08**.
