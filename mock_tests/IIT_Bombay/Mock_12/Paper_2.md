#### Question 1

- (A) $L^2 > 2mk$
- (B) $L^2 < 2mk$
- (C) $L^2 = 2mk$
- (D) $L = \sqrt{mk}$

#### Question 2

- (A) $\frac{Q^2}{32\pi\varepsilon_0 R^2}$
- (B) $\frac{Q^2}{16\pi\varepsilon_0 R^2}$

#### Question 3

- (A) $99.9\%$
- (B) $50.0\%$
- (C) $10.0\%$

#### Question 4

#### Question 5

#### Question 6

#### Question 10

#### Question 11

#### Question 12
