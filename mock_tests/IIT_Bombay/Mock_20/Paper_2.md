# PART I: PHYSICS (54 Marks)

## Section 1: One or More Than One Correct Questions (Questions 1 to 8)

#### Question 1
A uniform solid sphere of mass $M$ and radius $R$ is given a purely translational velocity $v_0$ without any initial rotation and placed on a rough horizontal surface with friction coefficient $\mu$. Which of the following statements is/are correct?
- (A) The time taken before pure rolling begins is $t_0 = \frac{2v_0}{7\mu g}$.
- (B) The velocity of the center of mass at the start of pure rolling is $v = \frac{5}{7} v_0$.
- (C) The total mechanical work done by friction during the transition to pure rolling is $-\frac{1}{7} M v_0^2$.
- (D) The angular momentum of the sphere about any point on the horizontal contact surface is conserved throughout the motion.

#### Question 2
A parallel plate capacitor with plate area $A$ and separation $d$ is charged to a potential difference $V_0$ and then disconnected from the battery. A dielectric slab of thickness $d/2$, area $A$, and dielectric constant $K = 3$ is inserted between the plates. Which of the following statements is/are correct?
- (A) The new capacitance is $\frac{3}{2} C_0$, where $C_0 = \varepsilon_0 A / d$.
- (B) The potential difference across the capacitor decreases to $\frac{2}{3} V_0$.
- (C) The electrostatic energy stored in the capacitor decreases by $\frac{1}{6} C_0 V_0^2$.
- (D) The electric field inside the dielectric slab is $\frac{V_0}{3d}$.

#### Question 3
An ideal gas undergoes a cycle on a $P-T$ plane consisting of an isobaric process, an isochoric process, and an isothermal process. If the heat capacity at constant volume is $C_V$, which of the following is/are correct?
- (A) In the isochoric process, the ratio of heat exchanged to work done is undefined because work is zero.
- (B) In the isothermal process, the change in internal energy is identically zero.
- (C) In the isobaric process, the fraction of heat converted to work is $R / C_P$.
- (D) The total change in enthalpy over one complete cycle is zero.

#### Question 4
A long solenoid of radius $a$ carrying a steady current produces a magnetic field $\vec{B}(t) = B_0 \cos(\omega t)\hat{k}$ inside the solenoid ($r < a$) and zero outside. A circular conducting wire loop of radius $b > a$ and resistance $R$ is placed coaxially around the solenoid. Which of the following statements is/are correct?
- (A) The induced EMF in the outer loop is $\pi a^2 B_0 \omega \sin(\omega t)$.
- (B) The induced electric field at radius $r > a$ is $\frac{a^2 B_0 \omega}{2r} \sin(\omega t)$.
- (C) The net magnetic flux through the outer loop at any instant is $\pi b^2 B_0 \cos(\omega t)$.
- (D) The average power dissipated in the outer loop over one full period is $\frac{\pi^2 a^4 B_0^2 \omega^2}{2R}$.

#### Question 5
Two point masses $m$ and $2m$ are connected by a light spring of stiffness $k$ and natural length $L_0$, resting on a frictionless horizontal floor. The mass $2m$ is suddenly given a velocity $v_0$ directed away from $m$ along the spring line. Which of the following statements is/are correct?
- (A) The velocity of the center of mass of the system is $\frac{2}{3} v_0$.
- (B) The maximum extension of the spring is $v_0 \sqrt{\frac{2m}{3k}}$.
- (C) When the spring has maximum extension, both masses move with identical velocity $\frac{2}{3} v_0$.
- (D) The mechanical energy of the system in the center of mass frame is $\frac{1}{3} m v_0^2$.

#### Question 6
A ray of light is incident from a medium of refractive index $\mu_1 = 1.60$ onto a medium of refractive index $\mu_2 = 1.20$. Which of the following statements is/are correct?
- (A) The critical angle for total internal reflection is $\theta_c = \sin^{-1}(0.75)$.
- (B) If the angle of incidence is $\theta_i = \sin^{-1}(0.75)$, the Brewster angle condition is also satisfied.
- (C) When total internal reflection occurs, there is an evanescent wave penetrating a short distance into the rarer medium without net energy transport across the boundary.
- (D) If the incident light is unpolarized and incident at Brewster's angle, the reflected light is completely plane-polarized.

#### Question 7
A photon of initial wavelength $\lambda_0 = 0.024\text{ \AA}$ undergoes Compton scattering through an angle $\theta = 90^\circ$ from a stationary free electron. Which of the following statements is/are correct? [Compton wavelength $\lambda_C = 0.0242\text{ \AA}$]
- (A) The wavelength of the scattered photon is approximately $0.0482\text{ \AA}$.
- (B) The kinetic energy transferred to the recoil electron is approximately $257\text{ keV}$.
- (C) The recoil electron moves at an angle $\phi = \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{\lambda_0}{\lambda_C + \lambda_0}\right)$ with the incident photon direction.
- (D) Total relativistic four-momentum is conserved during the collision.

#### Question 8
A simple pendulum consisting of a small bob of mass $m$ suspended by a light string of length $L$ is released from rest at an angle $\theta_0 = 60^\circ$ with the vertical. At the lowest point of its swing:
- (A) The tension in the string is $2mg$.
- (B) The radial acceleration of the bob is $g$.
- (C) The tangential acceleration of the bob is zero.
- (D) The angular momentum of the bob about the suspension point has magnitude $m L \sqrt{g L}$.

## Section 2: Numerical Value Questions (Questions 9 to 14)

#### Question 9

[Take $\pi = 3.14$].

#### Question 10

#### Question 11

Calculate the net rate of radiant heat loss $P_{\text{net}}$ (in Watts) from the body. [Take Stefan-Boltzmann constant $\sigma = 5.67\times 10^{-8}\text{ W}/(\text{m}^2\cdot\text{K}^4)$].

#### Question 12

A parallel beam of light is incident from the left onto the convex lens. Find the distance $s$ (in cm) from the concave lens where the emergent rays converge to form a real focus.

#### Question 13

#### Question 14
