#### Question 1 (Mechanics + Electrodynamics Synthesis)

In addition, a time-dependent spatially uniform electric field $\vec{E}(t) = E_0 \sin(\omega t)\hat{j}$ is established in the horizontal plane, where $E_0 = 4.00\times 10^3\text{ V/m}$ and $\omega = 10.00\text{ rad/s}$. The rod is initially at rest along the x-axis ($\theta = 0$) at $t = 0$. Calculate the maximum angular speed $\omega_{\text{max}}$ (in rad/s) achieved by the rod in its subsequent motion.

#### Question 2 (Thermodynamics — Real Gas Phase Change & Boundary Work)

The specific volume of saturated steam at $373.0\text{ K}$ is $v_g = 1.673\text{ m}^3/\text{kg}$, and the latent heat of vaporization is $L_v = 2.257\times 10^6\text{ J/kg}$. Calculate the change in internal energy $\Delta U$ of the water system during this complete vaporization process (in kJ).

#### Question 4 (Electromagnetic Induction — Rotating Coil with Self-Inductance)

#### Question 5 (Wave Optics — Interference with Tilted Thin Dielectric Wedge)

#### Question 6 (Electrostatics — Concentric Spherical Dielectric Shells with Boundary Polarization)

#### Question 7 (Rotational Dynamics — Complex Impact & Slip on Moving Plank)
