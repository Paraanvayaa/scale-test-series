## 🏛️ IIT Style Clusters & Distinctive Historical Signatures

### 1. IIT Bombay (`IIT_BOMBAY_STYLE` · Mocks 01–03)
- **Historical Reference**: 2007, 2015, 2022
- **Signature Features**:
  - High multi-concept inter-branch questions (38.8% multi-concept share).
  - Heavy thermodynamic cycles, enthalpy loops, and cyclic engine efficiency.
  - Complex non-uniform electromagnetic fields coupled with rotating bodies.
  - Rigorous functional equations and inequalities in mathematics.
  - Severe negative marking shock profiles (+4/-2).

### 2. IIT Delhi (`IIT_DELHI_STYLE` · Mocks 04–06)
- **Historical Reference**: 2008, 2013, 2020
- **Signature Features**:
  - Multi-correct dominance (31.3% historical share), where every single option ($A, B, C, D$) acts as an independent sub-problem requiring separate mathematical or conceptual verification.
  - Circuit transients ($RL, RC, LCR$) with switching discontinuities.
  - Stereochemical isomer counting (enantiomers, diastereomers, meso forms).
  - Calculus-heavy coordinate geometry and vector-matrix transformations.

### 3. IIT Guwahati (`IIT_GUWAHATI_STYLE` · Mocks 07–09)
- **Historical Reference**: 2010, 2016, 2023
- **Signature Features**:
  - Conceptual elegance: symmetry invariants and conservation laws defeat tedious algebraic brute-force.
  - High Modern Physics (matter waves, Bohr transitions, radioactive decay) and Thermodynamics share ($\ge 35\%$).
  - Physical chemistry equilibrium and colligative properties.
  - Vectors, 3D geometry, and coordinate geometry symmetry.

### 4. IIT Kanpur (`IIT_KANPUR_STYLE` · Mocks 10–12)
- **Historical Reference**: 2001, 2007, 2011, 2018
- **Signature Features**:
  - Extreme numerical and computational intensity ($\ge 38\%$ numerical questions).
  - Multi-stage ionic and chemical equilibria (amphiprotic salts, simultaneous solubility, complex buffer systems).
  - Spatial volume/surface integrations and differential equations in physics.
  - Definite integrals with non-trivial properties, Riemann sum conversions, and recursive sequence analysis.

### 5. IIT Kharagpur (`IIT_KHARAGPUR_STYLE` · Mocks 13–14)
- **Historical Reference**: 2006, 2014, 2021
- **Signature Features**:
  - Marked polarization and structural asymmetry between Paper 1 and Paper 2.
  - Detailed, descriptive problem statements (longest Chemistry average word count: 64.2 words).
  - Multi-step organic reaction cascades and rearrangement mechanisms (Pinacol, Beckmann, Benzilic acid, Hofmann).
  - Electrostatics with non-uniform distributed charge densities and non-conducting dielectric boundaries.

### 6. IIT Madras (`IIT_MADRAS_STYLE` · Mocks 15–17)
- **Historical Reference**: 2003, 2010, 2017, 2024
- **Signature Features**:
  - Electromagnetism hegemony ($\ge 37\%$ of Physics: LC oscillations, motional EMF, Hall effect, wave optics).
  - Fast single-correct velocity and crisp, non-wordy stem formulations.
  - Complex numbers on the Argand plane ($|z - z_0|$, locus intersections) and conic sections (tangents, normals, director circles).
  - Inorganic coordination chemistry (crystal field splitting, optical isomerism, Werner's theory).

### 7. IIT Roorkee (`IIT_ROORKEE_STYLE` · Mocks 18–20)
- **Historical Reference**: 2009, 2019
- **Signature Features**:
  - Classical Mechanics hegemony ($\ge 45\%$ of Physics: rolling without slipping on moving surfaces, variable-mass systems, compound pendulums, hydrodynamics/capillarity).
  - Realistic laboratory/experimental measurement setups and physical setups.
  - Analytical coordinate geometry (straight lines, circles, parabolas) and differential calculus (Rolle's theorem, LMVT, critical point analysis).
  - Chemical kinetics and solid state packing geometry.
