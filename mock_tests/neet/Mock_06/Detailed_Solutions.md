### Question 163
**Subject:** Zoology | **Section:** Section A | **Chapter:** Evolution (Hardy-Weinberg Principle Calculation)
**Question Type:** Direct MCQ
**Correct Answer:** Option **(4)**

#### Step-by-Step Pedagogical Explanation:

2. Genotype frequencies follow binomial expansion: $p^2 + 2pq + q^2 = 1$.
3. Frequency of homozygous dominant ($p^2$): $0.8^2 = 0.64$.
4. Frequency of homozygous recessive ($q^2$): $0.2^2 = 0.04$.
5. Frequency of heterozygous carriers ($2pq$): $2 \times 0.8 \times 0.2 = 0.32$ (or $32\%$).

#### Distractor & Option Deconstruction:

**Key NCERT Principle / High-Yield Takeaway:** Carrier frequency in Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium is $2pq = 2(0.8)(0.2) = 0.32$ (32%).
