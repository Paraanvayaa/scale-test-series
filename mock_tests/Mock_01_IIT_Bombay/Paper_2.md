## Section 1: Numerical Value Questions (Questions 1 to 8)

#### Question 1 (Mechanics — Rotating Liquid Surface)
A cylindrical vessel of radius $R = 0.20\text{ m}$ containing an incompressible liquid of density $\rho = 1000\text{ kg/m}^3$ is rotated about its vertical axis of symmetry with a constant angular velocity $\omega = 14.0\text{ rad/s}$. Due to rotation, the free liquid surface assumes a paraboloid of revolution. Find the difference in liquid height $h$ (in cm) between the edge and the lowest point on the axis. [Take $g = 9.80\text{ m/s}^2$].

#### Question 2 (Electrodynamics — LC Oscillations & Stored Energy)
In an ideal $LC$ oscillation circuit, the maximum electric potential difference across a capacitor of capacitance $C = 25.0\ \mu\text{F}$ is $V_0 = 120.0\text{ V}$. At an instant when the energy stored in the electric field of the capacitor is equal to the energy stored in the magnetic field of the inductor, find the instantaneous current $I$ (in Amperes) flowing through an inductor of inductance $L = 4.00\text{ mH}$.

#### Question 3 (Thermodynamics — Polytropic Process Work)
Two moles of a diatomic ideal gas ($\gamma = 1.40$) undergo a polytropic expansion according to the process equation $P V^{1.50} = \text{constant}$ from an initial state $(P_1 = 4.00\times 10^5\text{ Pa}, V_1 = 0.010\text{ m}^3)$ to a final volume $V_2 = 0.040\text{ m}^3$. Calculate the work done $W$ by the gas during this expansion (in kJ).

#### Question 4 (Wave Motion — Doppler Effect with Non-Collinear Motion)
A stationary acoustic siren emits a sound of frequency $f_0 = 600.0\text{ Hz}$. An observer travels in a straight car path at a constant speed $v_{\text{obs}} = 30.0\text{ m/s}$ along a line whose perpendicular distance to the siren is $d = 40.0\text{ m}$. At the instant when the observer is at a distance of $50.0\text{ m}$ from the siren and approaching it, find the apparent frequency $f'$ (in Hz) detected by the observer. [Speed of sound in air $v = 330.0\text{ m/s}$].

#### Question 5 (Electrostatics — Spherical Capacitor with Inhomogeneous Dielectric)
A concentric spherical capacitor consists of two thin conducting shells of radii $a = 10.0\text{ cm}$ and $b = 20.0\text{ cm}$. The intervening space is filled with an inhomogeneous dielectric whose relative permittivity varies radially as $\varepsilon_r(r) = \frac{k_0}{r}$, where $k_0 = 0.150\text{ m}$. Calculate the capacitance $C$ of the capacitor (in pF). [Take $\varepsilon_0 = 8.85\times 10^{-12}\text{ F/m}$].

#### Question 6 (Magnetism — Magnetic Field on the Axis of a Finite Solenoid)
A tightly wound finite solenoid has length $L = 30.0\text{ cm}$, radius $R = 4.0\text{ cm}$, and carries a steady current $I = 2.50\text{ A}$ with $n = 1000\text{ turns/m}$. Find the magnitude of the magnetic field $B$ (in mT) at a point on the solenoid axis located exactly at one of its end faces. [Take $\mu_0 = 4\pi \times 10^{-7}\text{ T}\cdot\text{m/A}$].

#### Question 7 (Optics — Resolving Power & Diffraction Limit)
A astronomical telescope objective lens of aperture diameter $D = 25.4\text{ cm}$ collects light from two distant point stars emitting light of wavelength $\lambda = 550.0\text{ nm}$. According to Rayleigh's criterion, calculate the minimum angular separation $\theta_{\text{min}}$ (in micro-radians, $\mu\text{rad}$) between the two stars for them to be just resolved.

#### Question 8 (Modern Physics — Compton Scattering)
A high-energy X-ray photon of wavelength $\lambda_0 = 0.0243\text{ nm}$ undergoes Compton scattering off a stationary free electron. If the photon is scattered through an angle $\theta = 90.0^\circ$, calculate the kinetic energy $K_e$ transferred to the recoil electron (in keV). [Take Compton wavelength of electron $\lambda_C = \frac{h}{m_e c} = 0.00243\text{ nm}$, $h c = 1.240\text{ keV}\cdot\text{nm}$].
