- **Q1**: `A, B, C, D`
  - Plank translation: $F - f_s = M a_p$.
  - Sphere translation: $f_s = M a_s$.
  - Sphere rotation: $f_s R = I_{\text{cm}} \alpha = \frac{2}{5} M R^2 \alpha \implies R \alpha = \frac{5 f_s}{2 M} = \frac{5}{2} a_s$.
  - No slipping condition at contact: $a_p = a_s + R \alpha = a_s + \frac{5}{2} a_s = \frac{7}{2} a_s$.
  - Substitute into plank equation: $F - M a_s = M \left(\frac{7}{2} a_s\right) = \frac{7}{2} M a_s \implies F = \frac{9}{2} M a_s$.
  - $a_s = \frac{2F}{9M}$, $a_p = \frac{7}{2} \left(\frac{2F}{9M}\right) = \frac{7F}{9M}$.
  - Static friction: $f_s = M a_s = \frac{2}{9} F$.
  - Maximum available friction: $f_s \le \mu_s M g \implies \frac{2}{9} F \le \mu_s M g \implies F \le \frac{9}{2} \mu_s M g$. All four options are correct.
